Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)? +
We cater primarily to B2B buyers, boutique owners, and wholesalers. Our MOQ varies by product category, but we offer flexible initial quantities to support new global partners.
How do you ensure safe international shipping for fragile items? +
All fragile items (marble, brass, pottery) undergo multi-layer bubble wrapping, thermocol padding, and custom export-grade corrugated boxes or fumigated wooden crates to guarantee damage-free transit.
What are your accepted payment terms for global orders? +
We accept secure international bank wire transfers (TT) and verified business payment channels as per global export norms.
Do you provide custom branding or private labeling? +
Yes, we offer custom packaging, private labeling, and bespoke handicraft manufacturing as per your brand specifications upon bulk order confirmation.
